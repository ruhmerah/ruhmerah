RUHMERAH Anti-Tarnish FAQs

Everything you need to know about caring for and enjoying your RUHMERAH jewellery.

Will RUHMERAH jewellery last forever?

No jewellery can remain unchanged forever, but our anti-tarnish jewellery is designed for long-lasting wear. With everyday use, it can generally last around 4–8 months, depending on usage and exposure to sweat, water, perfumes and chemicals. With occasional wear and proper care, it can last 2 years or even longer.

What does “anti-tarnish” actually mean?

Anti-tarnish jewellery has a protective coating that helps slow down tarnishing and preserves the jewellery’s shine and finish for longer. However, it is not completely immune to wear, moisture or chemicals.

I want to wear my jewellery every day. Is that okay?

Absolutely. RUHMERAH pieces are made to be enjoyed as part of your everyday style. Just remember that daily wear means more exposure to sweat, moisture and friction, which can naturally affect the finish over time.

Does wearing it occasionally make it last longer?

Yes. Jewellery that is worn occasionally and stored properly experiences much less exposure to sweat, moisture and everyday friction. With good care, your piece can last 2 years or even longer.

Is anti-tarnish jewellery waterproof?

Anti-tarnish does not mean completely waterproof. We recommend avoiding prolonged exposure to water, especially during bathing, swimming or water-based activities, as repeated exposure can gradually affect the protective finish.

What should I avoid while wearing RUHMERAH jewellery?

Try to keep your jewellery away from perfumes, lotions, chemicals, excessive sweat, chlorine and prolonged moisture. These can gradually affect the coating and shine.

Should I put on perfume before or after my jewellery?

Always perfume first, jewellery last. Allow perfumes, lotions and other beauty products to dry before putting on your jewellery. This simple habit can help extend the life of the finish.

What if my jewellery accidentally gets wet?

Don’t worry. Simply wipe it gently with a soft, dry cloth and allow it to dry completely before storing it. Occasional accidental contact with water is very different from repeated or prolonged exposure.

How should I store my RUHMERAH jewellery?

Store your jewellery in a clean, dry and preferably airtight pouch or jewellery box, away from moisture and direct sunlight. Keeping pieces separated also helps prevent scratches and tangling.

How do I keep the shine looking new for longer?

A little care goes a long way. After wearing your jewellery, gently wipe it with a soft, dry cloth and store it away from moisture. Avoid abrasive cleaners, polishing liquids and harsh chemicals.

Why can anti-tarnish jewellery change colour over time?

The anti-tarnish coating helps protect the jewellery, but it naturally experiences wear with use. Frequent contact with sweat, water, perfumes, chemicals and friction can gradually affect the finish. The lifespan therefore varies from person to person.

How long can I expect my RUHMERAH jewellery to last?

There is no fixed lifespan because it depends on how often you wear it and how you care for it. As a general guide, daily wear may last around 4–8 months, while jewellery worn occasionally and cared for properly can last 2 years or longer.

What is the difference between 316L and 304L stainless steel?

Both 316L and 304L are grades of stainless steel commonly used in jewellery and are known for their durability and corrosion resistance. The main difference is their composition: 316L contains molybdenum, which gives it greater resistance to corrosion, particularly in harsher or more humid environments.

304L is also durable and corrosion-resistant and is widely used for jewellery and everyday products. At RUHMERAH, some pieces may be made using 316L and others using 304L, depending on the design and product specifications.
